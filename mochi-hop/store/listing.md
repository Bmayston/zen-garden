**Full description (max 4000):**

Meet Mochi: a squishy little rice cake with a sprout on its head and a cheeky wink. 🌸

Tap anywhere to hop through a cherry-blossom zen garden, between skewers of pink, white and green
dango. Sounds easy? Mochi is sticky. Touch a skewer and… splat. 🍡

🌸 ONE TAP, ENDLESS FUN
Simple to learn and hard to master. One more go is only one tap away.

✨ RISK IT FOR PETALS
Scrape past skewer tips for close-call bonuses, grab golden gaps worth 3 points, and survive the
night winds as the garden turns from day to sunset to starry night.

🎀 COLLECT 13 FRIENDS
Sakura, Matcha, Sesame, Ichigo, Golden and more, plus seasonal friends who only visit at certain
times of year. Each one has its own look and hop.

🏆 DAILY CHALLENGE
A new course every day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak going and compare scores.

💌 CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS
Share a "Beat my score" card. Your friends can play your exact course right in their browser,
no install needed.

🎯 MISSIONS AND MEDALS
Daily and weekly missions, Bronze to Jade medals, and plenty of petals to earn.

♿ PLAY YOUR WAY
Separate music and sound volume, vibration on or off, reduced motion and a left-handed layout.

No accounts. No ads in the middle of a run. Just hop. 🍡
